Master's thesis · 2025

Flood Detection and Monitoring in Edge-AI

Indra Deimos, Madrid · CISESRES project with the European Space Agency · Supervisors: Marcos Quintana González, Prof. Jesús Bescós Cano

A flood detection and monitoring system built from hyperspectral imagery and geospatial data (DEM and DTM). The goal was to run the models on radiation-hardened hardware, so the design focuses on efficiency as well as accuracy.

  • Hybrid deep learning segmentation models (ResNet50 + UNet and ResNet50 + DeepLabv3+) deployed on a Xilinx FPGA, with state-of-the-art optimisation results.
  • Analysis of flood dynamics using high-resolution hyperspectral and Earth observation data.
  • Integration with hardware engineers for deployment in space-ready environments.

Tutored research project · 2024–2025

Explainable AI for Egocentric Video

IPCV Erasmus Mundus, University of Bordeaux · Supervisors: Prof. Jenny Benois-Pineau, Prof. Marcos Escudero-Viñolo

Improving object recognition and localization in first-person video by making the detector's decisions visible and using that feedback to refine detections.

  • Fine-tuned YOLOv8 on the Grasping in the Wild (GITW) dataset.
  • Applied EigenCAM, EigenGradCAM and feature explanation methods to interpret multiple detection heads.
  • Refined detections by iteratively finding and re-examining critical regions, improving accuracy and robustness.
