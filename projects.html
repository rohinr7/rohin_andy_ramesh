Projects & Research

Master's Thesis: Flood Detection and Monitoring in Edge-AI

Conducted at Indra Deimos with the European Space Agency (CISESRES project), 2025.

  • Built a flood detection and monitoring system from hyperspectral imagery and geospatial data (DEM, DTM).
  • Deployed hybrid deep learning segmentation models (ResNet50+UNet, ResNet50+DeepLabv3+) on a Xilinx FPGA for space-ready hardware.
  • Worked with hardware engineers on system integration for radiation-hardened environments.

Research Project: Explainable AI for Egocentric Video

Tutored research project at the University of Bordeaux, 2024–2025.

  • Fine-tuned YOLOv8 on the Grasping in the Wild (GITW) dataset.
  • Applied explainability methods (EigenCAM, EigenGradCAM, feature explanation) to interpret detection heads.
  • Refined detections by iteratively identifying critical regions, improving accuracy and robustness.
