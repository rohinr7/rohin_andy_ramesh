Featured work

From research to deployment

A selection of my thesis, research and engineering work in computer vision.

01

Flood Detection & Monitoring in Edge-AI

Master's thesis with Indra Deimos for the European Space Agency (CISESRES). Flood segmentation from hyperspectral imagery and elevation data, with hybrid ResNet50 models deployed on a Xilinx FPGA for space-ready hardware.

HyperspectralFPGAUNetDeepLabv3+Earth observation
02

Explainable AI for Egocentric Video

Fine-tuned YOLOv8 on the Grasping in the Wild dataset, with EigenCAM and EigenGradCAM explanations to show what the detector looks at.

YOLOv8XAIEgocentric vision
03

Ship Detection from SAR

Built a labelled SAR dataset from Gaofen-3 and Sentinel-1 imagery, with 43,819 ship chips, and fine-tuned YOLOv8 for maritime monitoring.

SARYOLOv8Remote sensing
04

Camera-based Collision Avoidance for UAVs

Vision-based sense-and-avoid for unmanned aerial vehicles, with YOLOv8 trained and running in real time on a Jetson Xavier for autonomous flight.

Jetson XavierReal-time inferenceAutonomous flight
